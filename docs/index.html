Step 3: Easy German Cover Letters

Create German Cover Letters in Seconds

Writing formal German application letters (DIN 5008 standard) can be stressful. ZUGZWANG automatically creates customized letters for each company so you always make a great impression.

Apply to dozens of companies with tailored, professional German letters.

  • Auto-Fills Every Detail: The company name, manager name, job title, address, and date are filled in automatically.
  • Complete Application PDF: Bundles your cover letter, Lebenslauf (CV), and school certificates into a clean PDF.
