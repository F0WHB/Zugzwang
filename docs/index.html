Step 2: Get Hiring Manager Contacts

Deep Impressum & HR Hunter

Crawl company web pages down to three levels deep to find actual hiring managers, direct telephone lines, and verified email mailboxes instead of generic info@ addresses.

Reach actual decision-makers directly on their personal desk lines.

  • 1Impressum & HR Parsing: Strictly extracts German legal Impressum entity details, registered trade registers, and direct HR manager contacts.
  • 2Diagnostics & Log Stream: Real-time streaming console tracks HTTP network codes, active workers, and data throughput.
  • 3Multi-Format Exports: One-click export to Excel (.xlsx), CSV, JSON, or Plain Text with custom column mapping.

100% Client-Side Privacy

Encrypted SQLite App Memory

All leads, contact numbers, and search histories are saved in your local app_memory.db. No data ever leaves your computer.

Step 3: Auto-Generate Cover Letters

Personalized Anschreiben & Broadcast

Generate company-specific German motivation letters (Anschreiben) adhering to DIN 5008 standards. Pair dynamically with your Lebenslauf and dispatch via custom SMTP.

Apply to dozens of companies in minutes with tailored German letters.

  • 1Dynamic Placeholders: Injects contact person, position title, company name, address, and current date automatically.
  • 2Safe Rate-Limited SMTP: Humanized dispatch pacing (configurable intervals) protects your sender domain reputation.
  • Application Bundling: Merges digital signatures, Deckblatt, Anschreiben, CV, and certificates into a pristine PDF bundle.

No technical skills are required. ZUGZWANG is a standalone desktop application with a clean, visual interface just like any regular Mac or Windows program. You simply enter the job title and city you want to target, click Start, and watch direct employer contacts appear.
